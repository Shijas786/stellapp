# Presentation Script: Stellapp

This script is designed to guide you step-by-step as you demo the Stellapp website to your audience. It includes **visual actions** (what you should do on the screen) and the **spoken script** (what you should say in simple, confident English).

---

## 🎬 Part 1: The Hook & Introduction

**[Visual Action]**
* Keep the website at the very top of the page.
* Point to the big title: **"Chat. Build. Pay. On Stellar."**
* Let the animated typing text run on the phone mockup.

**[What to Say]**
> "Hello everyone, and welcome. Today, I am excited to present **Stellapp** — the world's first AI-powered, WhatsApp-native crypto wallet built on Stellar.
> 
> Cryptocurrency is powerful, but for the average person, wallets are too complicated. There are seed phrases, browser extensions, gas fees, and complex user interfaces.
> 
> With Stellapp, we change everything. We bring the entire power of the Stellar network and Soroban smart contracts directly into the app that billions of people already use every single day: **WhatsApp**."

---

## 📱 Part 2: Core Features (The Bento Grid)

**[Visual Action]**
* Scroll down slowly to the Bento Grid section (**"With Stellapp, crypto is simple."**).
* Pause at each card as you speak about it.

**[What to Say]**
> "Let me walk you through the core capabilities of Stellapp:
> 
> * **First, Instant Global Payments**: You can send money to any contact or phone number globally, instantly, with near-zero fees. If they have WhatsApp, they can receive money.
> * **Second, Seamless DeFi Access**: You can swap tokens and manage assets simply by text. Our enterprise backend handles the custodial wallet key management securely.
> * **Third, AI Smart Contract Deployment**: This is a game-changer. Users can describe a smart contract idea in natural language, and our AI assistant writes, compiles, and deploys it on Stellar's Soroban network right inside the chat.
> * **Fourth, Zero-Knowledge Privacy**: We support advanced ZK Privacy Pools and Confidential Transfers, allowing users to shield their balances and perform untraceable transactions.
> * **Fifth, Background Automation**: The bot manages alerts, DCA recurring payments, and transaction tracking in the background."

---

## ⚡ Part 3: Live Action Mockup (The Phone Demo)

**[Visual Action]**
* Scroll down to the **"Features in Action"** section.
* Click the tabs on the left/top of the mockup one-by-one to switch the chat screens.
* **Click Tab 1 (Sending Tokens)**. Watch the mockup type and send 10 USDC.
* **Click Tab 2 (Deploy Contracts)**. Point to the Rust compiler output in the chat.
* **Click Tab 3 (ZK Privacy Pool)**. Highlight the note deposit and private withdrawal.

**[What to Say]**
> "Now, let’s look at how this works in action. On the screen, you can see our interactive phone mockup:
> 
> 1. **[Point to Tab 1]** Here, a user types 'Send 10 USDC to shijas'. The AI parses this command, prompts for confirmation, executes the transaction on Stellar, and returns a successful transaction hash in seconds.
> 
> 2. **[Point to Tab 2]** If a user wants to build, they can type 'Deploy a basic escrow contract'. The AI writes the Rust code, compiles the WASM binary, and deploys it directly to Soroban.
> 
> 3. **[Point to Tab 3]** When using the ZK Privacy Pool, users deposit funds, and receive a private note. They can later withdraw those funds anonymously to a new address, completely breaking the on-chain link between sender and receiver.
> 
> This is Web3 made as easy as sending a message to a friend."

---

## 🛡️ Part 4: Privacy & Ledger Visibility

**[Visual Action]**
* Scroll down to the **"Ledger Visibility"** comparison section.
* Hover or point to the **Standard Transfer** card (Red/Orange indicators).
* Hover or point to the **ZK Privacy Pool** and **Confidential Transfer** cards (Purple/Green indicators).

**[What to Say]**
> "Let’s talk about privacy. On a standard block explorer, every transaction detail is visible to the public. Anyone can see your wallet address, the recipient, and the exact amount you sent.
> 
> Stellapp integrates two state-of-the-art privacy protocols:
> 
> * **ZK Privacy Pools**: Where your funds are mixed in an on-chain anonymity pool and withdrawn via private zero-knowledge proofs.
> * **Confidential Transfers**: Where the transaction amounts are completely encrypted on-chain, and zero-knowledge range proofs are used to verify solvency without revealing how much money you have.
> 
> This brings bank-grade confidentiality to the open ledger."

> 
> It bridges the gap between complex blockchain tech and simple user experience.
> 
> Thank you for your time. You can try the demo now by clicking 'Chat on WhatsApp'. I am happy to take any questions you have!"
